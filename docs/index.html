Your lang for google map

Published by Stringon Inc.

Choose the language used by Google Maps while keeping Chrome's display language unchanged. Use a temporary language for one tab, or save your preferred language for future Maps visits.

Customize up to six shortcuts and search the full language list. Japanese is the initial Maps default. The extension interface follows Chrome's display language, with six translations and English fallback.

Language preferences are stored locally. The extension processes Maps URLs on your device to apply your selection; Stringon Inc. does not receive those URLs through the extension. See the privacy policy for details.
