## Detailed description

Use Google Maps in the language you prefer, while keeping Chrome's menus and
settings in their current language.

Your lang for google map lets you choose a Maps language from a toolbar menu.
Keep a temporary choice for one tab or save your preferred language for future
Maps visits.

FEATURES

• Quick access to up to six languages in the order you choose.
• English, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, Korean, Spanish, and Vietnamese are
  the initial menu shortcuts.
• More opens a searchable list of language choices, including regional variants.
• With Persistent unchecked, your selection affects only the current tab until
  it closes or Chrome restarts.
• With Persistent checked, the next language you select becomes your saved default.
• Settings lets you change the default language and customize the six shortcuts.
• The extension interface follows Chrome's display language, with English,
  Japanese, Traditional Chinese, Korean, Spanish, and Vietnamese translations.
  Other interface languages fall back to English.

GETTING STARTED

After installation, Japanese is the initial default for Google Maps. To choose
another language, open Google Maps and click the extension's toolbar icon. You
can pin the icon from Chrome's extensions menu. Open Settings to change your
default and menu shortcuts.

The extension works on https://www.google.com/maps and its subpaths. It preserves
your place, search, and directions while setting the requested language. Other
Google domains are handled if they redirect to this address. Google controls
which translations and language variants are available; some place names and
user-written reviews may stay in their original language.

PRIVACY AND PERMISSIONS

The extension processes Maps URLs and tab identifiers locally to apply your
language selection. Maps URLs can contain searches, addresses, or routes. The
extension does not store a browsing log or send those URLs to Stringon Inc.
Language preferences are stored locally in your Chrome profile. There are no
analytics, ads, or developer-operated server connections.

Chrome grants access to www.google.com at the host level; the extension's
language rules are restricted to Maps page requests. Ordinary navigation still
sends your Maps URL directly to Google. See the linked privacy policy for details.
